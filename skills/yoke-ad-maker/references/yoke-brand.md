# Yoke creative reference

Snapshot from the Ad Studio source on 2026-10-29. A newer verified brand pack or product specification takes precedence. These are fallback creative rules, not a claim that a connected service exists.

## Product truth

Call the product **Yoke**, never Yoke GTM or YokeGTM in ads.

Current documented capabilities:
- LinkedIn prospecting from each rep's own profile: connection invites, profile views, follow-ups.
- InMail follow-up to open profiles when an invite goes unanswered.
- Drafted replies that users approve or edit; approval settings per playbook.
- A Today list for promised follow-ups, with snooze.
- Daily/hourly sending limits per LinkedIn account.
- Workspaces with teammates and sending profiles.
- Import of LinkedIn and Sales Navigator connections.

Do not invent list building, email outreach, buying signals, autonomous CRM updates, booked meetings, reply rates, numerical outcomes, customer quotes, or unlisted integrations. A hook about a rep spending time on a list must not imply that Yoke builds the list.

## Audience and voice

Speak to sales/BD managers, team owners, and owner-operators who already know their work. Their problems include uneven follow-through across reps, repetitive outreach work, and personal memory acting as the team's process.

Be politely punky: a colleague gently teasing a recognizable habit. Keep the joke on the behavior. Use plain words, one joke and one problem per ad. Avoid hype, em dashes, and "seamless", "leverage", "unlock", "supercharge", "game-changer".

Established concept directions (not proof of product outcomes):
- Your follow-up strategy is "remembering."
- "We should do more on LinkedIn."
- You'll follow up when things calm down. So, never.
- You connected. They accepted. Now what?
- Very impressive spreadsheet. Does it book meetings?

CTA: **See how Yoke works**. No offer or destination is approved in this snapshot; retrieve a current one or leave it unset.

## Visuals and export

From the existing renderer: warm ground `#F7F4EC`, ink `#111317`, coral `#F04B37`, lime `#CDF05B`, white `#FFFFFF`, muted `#686A63`, stone `#D8D5CD`. Use Instrument Sans where available; Inter or Arial are documented fallbacks. Keep text editable. Use the official lockups in `assets/` (`yoke-lockup.svg` on light, `yoke-lockup-reversed.svg` on dark), unmodified, or a newer approved logo.

Existing sizes: feed 1080×1350, square 1080×1080, vertical 1080×1920. Respect the requested placement; use generous margins and mobile-readable type.

Static: about 12 words maximum in the hook, one concise payoff naming a real capability. A product-like card may have up to four short rows. Do not present illustrative UI as a verified screenshot.

Infographic motion: roughly 10–16 seconds, 3–5 short readable beats and an end card. Check timing and playback; preserve source and poster.

Higgsfield or hybrid video: use the available connector only when connected and authorized. Keep overlay text and branding editable; preserve the original clips, prompts, job IDs, edit instructions, and final video. If unavailable, provide a pending production brief rather than claiming a render.
